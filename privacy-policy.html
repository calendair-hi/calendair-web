2. What the app does

CalendAIr helps people find meeting times that work for everyone. You sign in, connect calendars, invite others, and create meetings. Optional features include AI-assisted time suggestions, push notifications, in-app purchases, and (if you consent) analytics and ads.

3. How we use your calendar

This is the core of the product. Please read it before you connect a calendar.

When you connect Google Calendar, Outlook, or iCloud Calendar, our servers call that provider’s API. We do not use the iOS EventKit permission or the Android system calendar permission.

Existing events already in your calendar

  • We request event data so we can tell when you are busy (start and end time, and whether an event is cancelled, marked free, or declined by you).
  • We do not save the titles, descriptions, locations, or attendee lists of those existing events in our databases.
  • We do not show that existing event content to other CalendAIr users or to advertising partners.
  • That data is processed in memory on our servers for the availability request and then discarded (operational logs may still contain technical errors; they are not used to reconstruct your existing calendar).

Meetings you create in CalendAIr

  • We store the title, description, times, and the people you invite.
  • Invitees see that information in the app. Push notifications may include the meeting title and names.
  • When a meeting is accepted, we write title, description, times, and attendee emails into the connected calendars so everyone has the event in Google, Outlook, or iCloud.

Google Contacts (only if you connect Google Calendar)

  • We may look up or create a contact (email and display name) in a group named “CalendAIr Connections”.
  • Purpose: so calendar invitations from CalendAIr are less likely to be treated as unknown sender / spam.
  • We do not use your contacts for advertising.

Tokens and credentials

  • Google and Outlook: we store encrypted refresh tokens so we can keep the calendar connected.
  • iCloud: we store encrypted credentials needed for CalDAV access (Apple ID username plus the app-specific password you enter).
  • You can disconnect in the app at any time. You can also revoke access in your Google or Microsoft account, or delete the app-specific password in your Apple ID settings.

Disconnecting a calendar or deleting your CalendAIr account does not delete events that were already written to Google, Outlook, or iCloud. We only remove our own records. See the Terms of Service.

4. Other data we process

Account (sign-in with Google or Apple via AWS Cognito)
Email address (Apple may provide a Hide My Email relay address), name, profile photo URL, language preference, and a user ID.

Connections between users
Invite codes (they expire after about a day) and who connected with whom.

Device notifications
A push token, platform, and language, so we can send meeting updates.

Subscriptions
Whether you have Premium, expiry, store platform, and the identifiers the App Store / Google Play need so we can restore and validate purchases.

Feedback
What you send us in the in-app feedback form.

AI suggestions (Google Gemini)
If you type a natural-language request (for example “next week Tuesday afternoon”), we send that text, together with your time zone and app language, to Google Gemini so it can be turned into time windows. We do not send your calendar, your event titles, your name, or the names of the people you invite.
We log your request together with the interpretation we get back, linked to your user ID, so we can spot misreadings and improve how well the app understands you. These log entries are deleted after about 30 days, and we do not use them for advertising.

Usage counters
How many AI requests you have made in the current period, so we can apply the limits of the free and Premium plans.

App diagnostics and ads (only with your consent where required)
Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google AdMob. If you consent to ads, the advertising identifier of your device (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) and technical data about the ad request are processed by Google for ad delivery and, if you allow it, for personalised ads. On iOS we ask separately through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency dialog. Without consent, ads are non-personalised. Calendar content and meeting content are never sent to these products. You manage this in the app (consent / “Cookie settings”), and Premium removes ads entirely.

Data stored on your device
The app keeps a local copy of your profile (name, email, profile picture URL, subscription status), your calendar connection status, your language, your consent choices, and a pending invite code, so it can start without reloading everything. This stays on your device. Removing the app removes it.

Website
calendair.de is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.); GitHub processes your IP address and request data in its server logs to deliver the pages and defend against attacks. Fonts, icons and every other file are served from our own domain: the site embeds no third-party content, sets no cookies of its own, and uses no analytics or advertising cookies. An invite link contains the invite code and, if your friend shared it that way, their first name in the URL; that is only displayed in your browser and is not stored by us.

Security and operations
We keep technical logs (for example error messages, user ID, timestamps) to run, secure, and debug the service. We do not log calendar content, meeting titles, access tokens, or passwords. The one piece of content we do log is the scheduling request you type — see “AI suggestions” above.

5. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

  • Provide the account, meetings, calendar sync, invites, and Premium you asked for — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract).
  • Connect calendars and Contacts, and show ads/analytics where we ask you — Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent). You can withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Withdrawing calendar consent means disconnecting the calendar; it is not the same as deleting your whole account.
  • Security, abuse prevention, debugging, legal claims, and improving how well the AI understands scheduling requests — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests).
  • Legal obligations (for example tax or store accounting) — Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve user-facing CalendAIr features (availability, creating/updating CalendAIr meetings on your calendar, and the Contacts group described above).
  • We do not use Google Calendar or Contacts data to serve advertisements.
  • We do not sell that data.
  • We do not allow humans to read it unless you give affirmative consent, it is needed for security or to comply with law, or use is limited to internal operations such as debugging (aggregated or as needed to fix an issue you report).

Our use of Microsoft Graph data follows the Microsoft APIs Terms of Use and is limited to the Outlook calendar features described in this policy.

7. Who we share data with (processors / providers)

We use other companies to run the product. They process data on our instructions or as independent controllers for their own platforms (sign-in, stores, ads):

  • Amazon Web Services — hosting, database, authentication (Cognito), logs (primarily EU, Germany).
  • Google — sign-in, Calendar and Contacts APIs, Gemini, Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging), AdMob, Google Play billing.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, iCloud Calendar (CalDAV), App Store billing and subscription notifications.

We do not sell your personal data.

8. Transfers outside the EEA

Our own infrastructure is primarily in the EU. Google, Apple, Microsoft, and some AWS functions may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, we rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, an adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified), and similar safeguards.

9. How long we keep data

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise it.

Typical periods in the product today:

  • Account after you delete it in the app: about 90 days (anonymised, then removed). Sign-in at Cognito is disabled.
  • CalendAIr meetings: until about 21 days after the meeting ends.
  • Invite codes: about 25 hours.
  • Feedback: about 6 months.
  • Operational logs (including the AI requests described in section 4): about 30 days; we do not use them to build a profile of you.
  • Sign-in record: after you delete your account, your sign-in is disabled immediately so nobody can log in as you. The remaining sign-in entry (email address and provider ID) is removed together with the account data.

Meetings you created may remain visible to other participants until those periods elapse, even if you delete your account. Events already on Google, Outlook, or iCloud stay there unless you delete them in that calendar.

10. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent at any time.

In the app: disconnect calendars; delete your account under Profile. You can also request deletion without the app — see Delete your account.

By email: info@calendair.de. We do not currently offer a full automated data-export file; we will handle access requests manually.

You can complain to a supervisory authority. For our seat in North Rhine-Westphalia that is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW). You may also contact the authority in your country of residence.

11. Automated decision-making

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects for you or similarly significantly affects you (Art. 22 GDPR). The AI only converts the text you type into time windows; you always choose the slot yourself.

12. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS). Our databases run in the EU (AWS, Frankfurt) and are encrypted at rest. Calendar refresh tokens and iCloud app-specific passwords get an additional layer of encryption before they are stored. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will inform you as required by Art. 33 and 34 GDPR.

13. Children

CalendAIr is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to info@calendair.de and we will delete it.
